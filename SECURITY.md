# Security policy

We take the security of PageToVid and of our users' data seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.** Report it privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/PageToVid/docs/security/advisories/new),
or email **hello@pagetovid.com (subject: "Security")**.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you believe it has. You will get an
acknowledgement within **3 business days** and a status update at least every **7 days** until it is
resolved.

## Scope

- `pagetovid.com` and its subdomains, the MCP endpoint (`https://pagetovid.com/mcp`), the REST API,
  the browser and VS Code extensions.
- Out of scope: denial-of-service, social engineering, reports from automated scanners without a
  demonstrated impact, and third-party services we do not control.

## Safe harbour

Research done in good faith and within this policy — no data destruction, no access to other users'
data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue, no degradation of the service — will not be
pursued. Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosing it.
